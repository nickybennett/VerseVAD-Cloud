# VerseVAD Free Training Materials

This tracked folder contains the public learner-facing portion of the VerseVAD
training pathway:

- four independent-study learner manuals; and
- four applied exercises.

The four levels are VerseVAD Foundations, Analyst Level 1, Analyst Level 2,
and Authorized Instructor Training. They are available inside VerseVAD under
**Learn → Training** and from <https://www.versevad.org/training>.

Evaluator answer keys, scoring rubrics, candidate records, certificates, and
authorization materials are deliberately not included in the public
repository or application package. Completion or instructor authorization is
not conferred by downloading or completing these files alone.

These learner materials are distributed as part of VerseVAD under the
repository's GPL-3.0-only license. Third-party lexicons, datasets, papers, and
literary texts retain their own licenses and are not included in the course
documents.
